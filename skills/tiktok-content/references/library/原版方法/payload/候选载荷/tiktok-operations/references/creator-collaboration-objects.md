# Creator collaboration objects

## Name the object before planning outreach

“Influencer cooperation” is not one object. Keep these separate and verify current market/account availability:

| Object | Primary purpose | Commercial structure | Publishing/ads boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shop Affiliate Open Collaboration | make eligible products discoverable to a broad affiliate pool | seller-set commission and optional sample/terms | creator chooses whether to promote; platform acceptance is not paid-media permission |
| Shop Affiliate Target Collaboration | invite selected creators to named products/terms | targeted commission, sample, deadline or other current fields | invitation and creator acceptance are distinct states |
| TikTok One creator project | source/manage creator deliverables for a brand project | project fee/terms and deliverables | publishing identity, branded-content review, asset delivery, and Ads Manager sync must be recorded |
| direct collaboration | negotiate outside a platform-managed campaign | fee, commission, product, retainer, or hybrid | contract, disclosure, payment, tax, usage, and dispute terms require explicit ownership |
| organic content licence | reuse content in owned organic contexts | fee or included licence | does not imply paid advertising, editing, sublicensing, or perpetual use |
| Spark Ads authorization | use an eligible existing TikTok post as an ad identity/creative route | paid media plus any creator compensation/ads commission | authorization scope/expiry and underlying music/person/mark rights are separate |

Do not treat public visibility, product tagging, affiliate acceptance, a download, a delivery file, a TikTok One sync, or a Spark authorization code as universal ownership.

## Shared collaboration record

Use one record per creator × product/project × collaboration object:

```text
collaboration_id and object_type:
market/account/shop/project/product:
creator identity and audience-fit evidence:
inviter and publishing party:
status with timestamp:
fee/commission/ads commission/sample/other consideration:
deliverables, deadline, revisions and cancellation:
claim/disclosure requirements:
organic licence: surface, territory, duration, edit permission, sublicensing
paid licence/Spark: identity, advertiser, objective, territory, duration, expiry/revocation
music/person/trademark/product rights:
platform sync and object IDs:
result owner, attribution window and dedup rule:
refund-mature contribution:
issue, escalation, end/revocation evidence:
approver:
```

Fee, commission, ads commission, sample value, discounts, and media spend are different cost lines. Record them separately before calculating contribution.

## Workflow

1. Verify product/shop and creator eligibility, market, audience/product fit, account health, claims, disclosure, and sample capacity.
2. Choose exactly one collaboration object or explicitly link multiple records; do not overwrite Open terms with Target terms or merge direct and platform contracts.
3. Prepare an offline brief and rights matrix. Contact, invite, sample shipment, contract, payment, commission change, publication, and ads require their own authorization.
4. Track offer/invitation, acceptance, sample, receipt, draft/deliverable, review, publication, product link, disclosure, and licence as separate states.
5. Join content and order evidence only under the declared object/window; check overlap with own content, other creators, LIVE, Product GMV Max, and Spark/ordinary ads.
6. Evaluate retained orders, refunds, total collaboration/media cost, contribution, content learning, and relationship reliability.
7. Renew, revise, hold, end, or revoke access/rights with evidence and an owner.

## Rights decision tree

- **Creator will publish organically only:** record disclosure and exact organic-use rights; paid use remains `not_granted`.
- **Brand receives a file:** delivery does not grant posting, editing, music, person, mark, or paid-media rights; check contract.
- **TikTok One project syncs to Ads Manager:** verify the synced object, advertiser permission, review state, usage period, and underlying rights.
- **Spark requested:** verify post owner, creator approval, scope, expiry/revocation, music, people, marks, product claims, and spend approval.
- **Collaboration ends:** stop new use at expiry/revocation and trigger access, asset, and payment reconciliation.

> **B delta candidate — 2026-09-05; independent local retest pending.** For a post that loses its affiliate product link because that product’s collaboration ends, the official video-code note describes the linked ad continuing until code expiry. Keep collaboration state, ad permission/code expiry, product-change permission, and actual ad delivery separate; turning product changes off does not prove delivery stopped. Platform continuation is not contractual permission: stop new use on expiry/revocation or prohibited terms, and have the authorized owner verify existing ads and obtain a timestamped readback. Without that readback, delivery is `unknown`. Do not generalize to all Spark/TikTok One objects. ## Attribution and duplicate-cost control

For every order/result view, retain the collaboration object, creator/post, product, platform attribution owner, paid support, fee/commission lines, refund maturity, and a privacy-safe dedup key. Never add creator-attributed GMV, Spark/ordinary ad revenue, Product GMV Max GMV, and LIVE totals and call the sum incremental.

Keep a single creator success as `case_only`. Upgrade a working pattern only after safe, comparable repetition and current official compatibility; read [evidence-and-practitioner-method-admission.md](evidence-and-practitioner-method-admission.md).

## Rejections and stop conditions

Reject fake creators, bought or scraped identities, bulk unsolicited harassment, undeclared ads, fake reviews/orders, copied content, forced authorization, indefinite/ambiguous rights, evasion, and guaranteed earnings. Hold when identity, eligibility, product, claims, disclosure, terms, rights, sample capacity, data ownership, economics, or approver is missing.

For least-privilege partner access read [business-center-and-asset-governance.md](business-center-and-asset-governance.md). For creator content planning read [organic-account-and-content-growth.md](organic-account-and-content-growth.md).
