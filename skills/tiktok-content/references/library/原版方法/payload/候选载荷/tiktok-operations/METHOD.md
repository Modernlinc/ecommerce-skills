## Purpose

Turn a TikTok objective or evidence set into a compliant, measurable operating loop. Treat course claims, screenshots, third-party dashboards, and competitor examples as inputs to test—not as platform truth or guaranteed outcomes.

## Commercial-method scope

Prioritize the decisions that determine whether and how a product can be sold: market and customer, product/offer, pricing and contribution economics, listing and promotion, sales-content strategy, creator/affiliate/LIVE channels, ads and GMV Max, fulfillment/service, measurement, scaling, and stop conditions.

Do not turn this Skill into a tutorial library for CapCut/Jianying, AI image or video generation, digital-human tools, software installation, device setup, or button-by-button editing. When those sources appear in a learning corpus, retain only decision-relevant sales logic, evidence, rights, disclosure, or risk gates; otherwise classify them as non-core production how-to.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Default to planning, drafting, analysis, QA, and evidence organization.
- Do not log in, publish, message creators, change shop/account settings, spend money, alter inventory, handle payments, or open real customer/order data without explicit authorization for that action.
- Never provide ban evasion, fake-location, bulk-account automation, fake engagement, review manipulation, or enforcement-circumvention instructions.
- Never recommend downloading, re-uploading, or lightly editing another creator's work without documented permission. Abstract creative variables; create original or licensed assets.
- Do not promise reach, followers, orders, GMV, profit, payout, or account approval.
- Treat market access, creator eligibility, Shop policies, ad products, attribution, UI paths, fees, promotion rules, and thresholds as high-drift facts. Verify them from the target market's current official TikTok pages on the day of use.

Read [compliance-and-approval-gates.md](references/compliance-and-approval-gates.md) whenever the request touches accounts, Shop, creators, ads, commercial claims, or publishing.

## Operating loop

### 1. Define one business decision

Write the decision as a testable sentence, such as:

> For `[market + audience]`, can `[offer]` achieve `[observable action]` through `[content/Shop/creator/LIVE/ad path]` while staying above `[contribution-profit boundary]` within `[window]`?

If the goal is only “go viral” or “increase GMV,” translate it into an observable business decision before proposing tactics.

### 2. Establish eligibility and evidence

Check, in order:

1. market/product/account eligibility and account health;
2. content and commercial-claim compliance;
3. asset and creator authorization;
4. listing/landing/product consistency;
5. inventory, fulfillment, returns, and support capacity;
6. measurement definitions and economic boundary.

If an earlier layer fails, do not diagnose the problem as an algorithm or creative issue.

### 3. Build the smallest useful test

- Declare whether the test compares materially different customer/creative concepts or isolates one variable. Do not present a concept test as a clean single-variable result.
- State inputs, owner, output, evidence, observation window, budget/capacity limit, and stop condition.
- Prefer reversible drafts, synthetic examples, and offline analysis until the user authorizes live actions.
- Distinguish platform eligibility, content distribution, product interest, conversion, fulfillment, and profit; do not collapse them into one score.

### 4. Inspect results

Use the chain:

```text
eligibility/health
→ eligible impressions or distribution
→ attention and comprehension
→ product/profile action
→ checkout or lead
→ paid order
→ fulfilled order
→ retained contribution profit
```

At each break, list observed facts, plausible explanations, missing evidence, and the next discriminating test.

### 5. Decide and stop safely

Return one of:

- `continue`: evidence supports another controlled iteration;
- `revise`: the hypothesis or asset needs a bounded change;
- `hold`: evidence, rights, eligibility, capacity, or approval is missing;
- `stop`: policy, economics, account health, or repeated negative evidence makes continuation unsafe.
