# Organic account and content growth

## Decision boundary

Use this reference for account positioning, organic demand discovery, content portfolios, community feedback, account-level diagnosis, and review cadence. It does not teach filming, editing, AI generation, or a universal posting frequency.

No account access is implied. Without an authorized account export or readback, create an offline plan and mark live profile, publishing, comments, followers, traffic, and feature availability `unknown`.

## Account operating card

Before selecting topics, record:

| Field | Required decision |
|---|---|
| market and language | exact target market, audience language, timezone, and any regulated category |
| audience and job | who the account serves and the decision/problem it helps with |
| value promise | what a viewer can consistently expect without an outcome guarantee |
| business destination | Shop product, profile action, website, lead path, or education-only role |
| profile continuity | name, bio, proof, disclosure, link or showcase state; `unknown` until observed |
| content rights | owned/licensed people, product, music, marks, testimonials, and reuse scope |
| operating capacity | owners for research, approval, publishing, comments, service, and analytics |
| baseline | dated reach, search, profile, product, order, and quality evidence, or `unknown` |
| stop conditions | claims, rights, account health, harassment, service capacity, or economic boundary |

Do not use an account biography, niche label, or high-view competitor as proof of product demand.

## Build a demand ledger, not a trend list

Candidate demand may come from Creator Search Insights, Comment Insights, Creative Center, TikTok Studio/account analytics, customer-service questions, reviews, search terms, Shop analytics, or authorized research. Treat each source according to its evidence strength and market/date.

Use one row per demand candidate:

```text
demand_id:
market/language:
source and observed date:
viewer wording or job:
business relevance:
evidence strength: official_capability | account_observed | cross_source_signal | source_opinion
content hypothesis:
offer/destination:
rights or claim risk:
owner:
next discriminating test:
```

A trend becomes a topic hypothesis only when the audience, business job, available proof, rights, and destination align. Search demand and For You discovery demand may conflict; keep both visible instead of averaging them into one score.

## Content portfolio

Organize work at three levels:

1. **Pillar** — a durable customer problem, use case, belief, proof domain, or brand role.
2. **Series** — a repeatable promise with a recognizable question and evidence format.
3. **Episode** — one business question, one audience need, one proof path, and one next action.

Balance the portfolio by business job rather than a fixed daily quota:

| Job | Typical evidence | Useful downstream signal |
|---|---|---|
| discovery | search/question evidence, category context | eligible reach, search views, qualified comments |
| education | demonstration, comparison, process, limitation | retention, saves, comprehension questions |
| trust | provenance, test, customer-safe proof, objection answer | profile/product action, lower repeated objections |
| conversion | accurate offer, product match, disclosed CTA | product view, checkout, retained order |
| retention/community | follow-up, service explanation, recurring series | returning viewers, repeat questions, repeat orders |

Each content record should include `business_question`, `audience_need`, `pillar`, `series`, `hypothesis`, `proof`, `CTA`, `destination`, `claims`, `rights`, `owner`, `published_object_or_N/A`, `window`, `result_or_N/A`, and `next_decision`.

Publishing cadence follows audience opportunity and team capacity. A practitioner cadence is a testable hypothesis, not an algorithm rule.

## Weekly operating loop

1. **Collect:** add search, comment, customer, Shop, and performance evidence with date and market.
2. **Prioritize:** choose the smallest set of questions that connect audience need to an admissible offer.
3. **Brief:** define concept, proof, prohibited claims, rights, CTA, destination, owner, and intended learning.
4. **Approve:** obtain human approval for publishing or customer interaction; offline drafts remain unpublished.
5. **Observe:** join content, profile, product, Shop, order-quality, and contribution evidence without filling missing data.
6. **Diagnose:** locate the first break and distinguish eligibility, distribution, comprehension, action, conversion, fulfillment, and profit.
7. **Decide:** reuse the concept, revise one declared variable, hold for evidence/rights/capacity, or retire it.

## Organic diagnosis tree

Use the first applicable branch:

- **No/low eligible distribution:** verify post/account eligibility, disclosure, originality, rights, market and publication state before invoking “account weight.”
- **Reach but weak attention/comprehension:** test the audience question, first promise, proof order, or format; do not change all at once.
- **Attention but weak profile/product action:** inspect offer clarity, CTA, profile/showcase continuity, and audience–product fit.
- **Product visits but weak orders:** inspect Listing, price/offer, trust, inventory, fulfillment, and checkout—not only the video.
- **Orders but weak retained contribution:** inspect discounts, commission, ads overlap, cancellations, refunds, fulfillment, and costs.
- **One spike with no repeat:** keep as `case_only` until a comparable concept works again under a declared context.

Views, followers, saves, or comments alone cannot select a commercial winner.

## Community and reuse

- Classify comments as question, objection, product/fit signal, service issue, misinformation, abuse, or policy risk.
- Draft responses from verified facts; a human owns publishing, refunds, disputes, sensitive data, and policy-sensitive claims.
- Turn repeated questions into the demand ledger and Listing/service changes, preserving source and date.
- Reuse only content with documented ownership/licence, person/music/mark permissions, disclosure continuity, and destination accuracy.
- Refresh changed prices, inventory, claims, product variants, promotions, and policy-dependent instructions before reuse.

Never use fake engagement, argument bait that misleads, bought accounts, location/identity evasion, copied clips, or comment manipulation as growth methods.
