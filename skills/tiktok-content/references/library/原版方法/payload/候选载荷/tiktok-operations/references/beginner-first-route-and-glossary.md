# TikTok 新手第一条卖货路线与名词表

Use this page first when the request is broad, such as “这个产品怎么在 TikTok 卖”。The goal is to reach one safe, inspectable offline recommendation without opening an account, publishing, contacting creators, spending, changing inventory, or handling customer data.

## Six-step first route

### 1. Write one business decision

```text
market and audience:
product and offer:
observable action:
contribution-profit boundary:
decision window:
```

If any field is missing, write `unknown`; do not invent a benchmark.

Use this minimum concept card（概念卡）before discussing formats or channels:

```text
customer problem or desired progress:
product mechanism and one truthful proof:
one sales concept (what the viewer should understand):
one permitted CTA and destination:
claims and rights evidence:
target event and denominator:
refund-maturity window:
known inputs / unknown inputs:
```

This is an offline sales concept, not a generated video, published post, or permission to execute. One completed concept card is the minimum offline output; three cards form a full candidate pack for comparison, not a mandatory minimum before the route can be assessed.

### 2. Fill the minimum intake

Record only what changes the first route: target market, Shop/account state, product eligibility, proof and prohibited claims, price and variable costs, inventory/fulfillment/returns, owned or licensed assets, target event/measurement, capacity, budget ceiling, owner, and approval scope.

### 3. Choose one primary route

| If the product most needs… | First route to assess | First offline output |
|---|---|---|
| a clear demonstration or problem/solution explanation | owned shoppable content（offline candidate） | at least one original sales-concept card plus claim/rights checks; three cards form the full candidate pack. This remains an offline operating proposal, and Shop/content eligibility is `unknown` until an authorized account readback |
| borrowed trust or many credible demonstrations | creator/affiliate | creator criteria, economics, brief, rights fields, outreach draft |
| live demonstration and objection handling | Organic/Shoppable LIVE | run-of-show, roles, claim/stock/service checklist |
| traffic to a site or a non-Shop objective | ordinary Ads Manager | Campaign → Ad Group → Ad plan plus event/measurement checklist |
| selected Shop products need total-channel optimization for non-LIVE orders | Product GMV Max | Product object card plus non-LIVE paid/organic/affiliate reconciliation; do not treat it as LIVE-room optimization |
| paid optimization for a selected LIVE event | LIVE GMV Max | LIVE object card plus in-room/post-exit and lifecycle readback fields |

Do not start all routes at once. Choose one primary route and name the evidence that would justify adding another.

Before continuing, label the three roles separately so a dependency is not mistaken for a second route:

```text
one primary route: the single route being assessed now
required base objects: account / Shop / eligible product / LIVE event / measurement objects the route depends on
transaction destination: Shop listing / website / lead destination / other declared destination
```

For example, “owned shoppable content” can be the one primary route while Shop, an eligible product and an accurate Listing are required base objects and the Listing is the transaction destination. This does not authorize Shop as a second route or authorize publishing.

### 4. Run five admission checks

1. **Eligibility:** the market, account, Shop, product and chosen object are currently eligible—or marked `unknown`.
2. **Rights and claims:** product claims, music, people, trademarks and creator paid-use rights are documented.
3. **Fulfillment:** inventory, dispatch, returns, support and capacity can absorb the proposed volume.
4. **Profit:** one currency/window joins price, discounts, refunds, product, affiliate, ad, logistics, payment and return costs.
5. **Measurement:** the target event, denominator, attribution boundary, baseline and delayed/refund maturity are explicit. Until an authorized account readback, record the platform field name, counting rule, denominator and account availability as `unknown`; an offline metric label is only a placeholder.

If an earlier check fails, do not call it an algorithm or creative problem.

### 5. Produce the offline plan

```text
primary route:
required base objects:
transaction destination:
why it fits this product:
required evidence already present:
missing evidence:
one hypothesis:
one bounded change:
expected leading signal:
business outcome and contribution check:
capacity or maximum-loss ceiling:
maximum-loss components checked: cash / labor / opportunity cost / samples / discounts / cancellations and refunds / fulfillment / customer support
stop condition:
human owner and approval required:
online route status: hold
```

### 6. Inspect and stop safely

Check that the output names one route, preserves unknowns, separates platform attribution from contribution and incrementality, includes rights/fulfillment/measurement, and does not authorize a live action. Stop at `hold` whenever eligibility, rights, economics, measurement, budget, capacity or approval is missing. A finished plan is not permission to log in, publish, message, launch, spend, change stock, restart a campaign, or touch customer/payment data.

## Minimal glossary

| Term | Meaning in this Skill |
|---|---|
| Organic | unpaid account content and community activity; distribution is not guaranteed |
| Shop | TikTok Shop commerce objects such as products, listings, transactions, health and fulfillment |
| Creator/Affiliate | a Shop or direct creator collaboration with product, commission/fee, rights, disclosure and result records |
| TikTok One | a creator-project workflow with its own project, deliverable and advertising handoff states; not the same as Shop Affiliate |
| Spark | an ad route using an owned post or a creator post with explicit authorization; a public post is not paid-use permission |
| Organic/Shoppable LIVE | a live selling/education event without assuming paid LIVE optimization |
| ordinary Ads | TikTok Ads Manager Campaign → Ad Group → Ad for a named objective, destination and event |
| Product GMV Max | TikTok Shop total-channel optimization for selected products and non-LIVE orders; current platform attribution can include non-LIVE paid, organic and affiliate orders, but this route does not optimize a LIVE room |
| LIVE GMV Max | paid optimization for a selected LIVE room/event; in-room conversion and lifecycle states stay separate from Product GMV Max |
| Business Center | the member, partner, role and asset-permission layer; role names do not by themselves prove effective access |
| platform attribution | credit assigned under TikTok's reporting rule; not automatically retained profit or causality |
| Attribution Analytics | a TikTok reporting/analysis surface for attribution-window and conversion-path diagnostics where available; it does not by itself prove account availability, causal lift, refund-mature revenue, profit, or creative effectiveness |
| contribution profit | refund-mature net revenue minus all defined variable costs under one currency/window |
| incrementality | what likely happened because of the intervention, requiring a defensible comparison rather than a dashboard label |
| target event（目标事件） | the one observable action used to judge the route, such as qualified product-page view, add-to-cart, retained order, or qualified lead; name its data source |
| eligible views（合格观看） | a placeholder metric name only until the actual account/report readback identifies the exact platform field, counting rule and denominator; do not invent or compare it before then |
| denominator（分母） | the population or amount the metric divides by, such as eligible sessions, selected-product non-LIVE GMV, or ad cost; never compare metrics with different denominators. If the exact field or population has not been read back, keep the denominator `unknown` |
| refund maturity（退款成熟） | the point when the chosen order cohort has had enough time for cancellations, refunds and returns to be reflected; before then contribution remains provisional |
| gross margin（毛利率） | use only the definition supplied for the actual business and window; if the numerator, denominator or included costs are not supplied, keep the term `unknown` rather than assuming a standard formula |
| readback | a timestamped observation of the actual market/account object, setting, status or result after an authorized action; a plan or screenshot from a course is not readback |
| non-LIVE baseline | a selected-product non-LIVE reference measured before Product GMV Max starts, or built through a separately designed comparable time window, product group, or market group. Platform attribution is report assignment, not a causal counterfactual（不是因果反事实）; if comparability, interference, or sample sufficiency is weak, keep incrementality `unknown` |

When refund maturity is unknown, calculate only the refund-before-known-item arithmetic proxy（退款前已知项算术代理值）and label it explicitly:

```text
refund-before-known-item arithmetic proxy = known receipts or net-revenue proxy - known variable costs
missing cost items: ...
refund maturity: unknown
status: arithmetic proxy only / not contribution profit / not known-input preliminary difference
```

Only after refund-mature net revenue is known may the output be named known-input preliminary difference（已知项初步差额）:

```text
known-input preliminary difference = known refund-mature net revenue - known variable costs
missing cost items: ...
status: provisional / not contribution profit
```

For an organic route, maximum loss is not merely advertising cash. Use this checklist and keep every missing item `unknown`:

```text
approved cash spend:
labor:
opportunity cost:
samples:
discounts:
cancellations / refunds / returns:
fulfillment and reverse logistics:
customer support and service load:
total maximum-loss ceiling:
```

The total ceiling is incomplete while any applicable component is `unknown`; do not silently treat an omitted or non-cash component as zero.

Do not convert missing cost, refund, attribution, or comparison inputs to zero. 缺任一核心输入时必须停止在离线候选或 `hold`。

After this page, open no more than the three references needed for the chosen route: the relevant channel reference, [compliance-and-approval-gates.md](compliance-and-approval-gates.md), and—when ads or evidence sufficiency matters—[ads-measurement-and-experiments.md](ads-measurement-and-experiments.md) or [evidence-and-practitioner-method-admission.md](evidence-and-practitioner-method-admission.md). Under this limit, non-primary routes receive only an admission/stop check; do not call that an execution plan for creators, LIVE, Shop, ordinary Ads or GMV Max.

If a risky side path appears after those three references—such as account evasion, unlicensed reuse, sensitive data, spend, payment, inventory or live-account changes—apply only the applicable stop/hold gate from the current evidence. Mark the route plan `incomplete_due_to_side_path` and state what specialist review is missing. Do not open a fourth reference merely to make the route look complete, and do not convert a gate-only judgment into operational advice.
