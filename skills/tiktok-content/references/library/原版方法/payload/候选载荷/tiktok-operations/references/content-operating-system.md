# Content operating system

This reference governs how one content unit helps sell and how asset-level creative performance is diagnosed. It does not teach editing software, AI video tools, camera operation, or step-by-step asset generation. For account positioning, demand, pillars/series, portfolio cadence, community feedback, and account-level organic diagnosis, read [organic-account-and-content-growth.md](organic-account-and-content-growth.md).

## Build a content evidence library

Research competitor and adjacent content to identify variables—not to copy assets. Record:

- audience and viewing context;
- job, desire, problem, or objection;
- opening hook and first visible proof;
- demonstration, story, comparison, tutorial, review, or scene structure;
- pacing, shot sequence, on-screen text, audio role, CTA;
- comments that reveal questions, objections, vocabulary, and post-purchase risk;
- source URL/date and which elements are ideas versus protected expression.

Never download/repost merely because content is public.

## Localization and AI-assisted production gate

Use translation, dubbing, avatars, text-to-video, or other generative tools only on inputs the operator owns or is licensed to adapt. Before production, record the source, rights scope, target market/language, intended claim, and human reviewer. Preserve product facts and character/voice continuity across scenes; review meaning, cultural fit, pronunciation, visual defects, disclosure, and claim accuracy before any publication decision.

An AI-generated output is a candidate asset, not proof of copyright ownership, product truth, platform eligibility, or market fit. Do not use translation, cropping, re-editing, or generation as a method to disguise copied content or evade similarity and rights checks.

For `tiktok-operations`, stop at the production brief, evidence/rights requirements, review criteria, and measurement plan. Route tool-specific execution to a separate media-production capability only when the user explicitly asks for it.

## Choose a content role

Each post should primarily do one job:

- discoverability: name a problem, identity, situation, or curiosity;
- comprehension: show what the product/service is and how it works;
- proof: demonstrate, compare, document, or answer an objection;
- conversion: connect the proof to a specific, accurate next action;
- retention: create a repeatable series, progress arc, or audience ritual;
- service: answer questions, correct expectations, or reduce returns.

Match the form to the buying decision rather than to a universal viral template. A simple, visually demonstrable product may need a direct proof sequence; a higher-risk, unfamiliar, or comparison-heavy product may need teaching, objection handling, creator trust, or LIVE demonstration. The product should appear only as early as needed for honest comprehension—never use a fixed number of seconds as a guarantee.

## Test design

Choose the test type before producing variants:

- **concept test**: compare materially different customer situations, problems/desires, proof paths, or creative ideas. More than one creative element may differ, so the result selects a direction rather than proving which isolated element caused it;
- **variable test**: keep the concept, offer, page, audience/route, target event, and other creative elements stable, then change one main variable per comparison.

Use the smallest purposeful set that production and review capacity can support. Candidate variables include:

- hook angle;
- first proof frame;
- use case;
- demonstration order;
- creator/voice/format;
- CTA or offer framing.

If platform delivery gives variants materially different audiences, placements, exposure, or spend, record delivery contamination and downgrade the result to directional. Use a current platform split-test feature when clean isolation matters and is available; a platform-declared winner still must pass order quality, contribution economics, and fulfillment review.

Do not prescribe a universal daily posting count. Set cadence from production quality, moderation/support capacity, platform feedback, and the learning question.

## Diagnosis

| Observed break | Inspect next |
|---|---|
| not distributed/eligible | account health, policy, format, processing status, rights |
| impression but early exit | opening relevance, clarity, visual proof, pacing |
| watch but no action | audience/offer match, CTA, trust, link/product availability |
| product click but no checkout | listing consistency, price, shipping, reviews, variant, local relevance |
| order but high refund/negative VOC | product promise, quality, fulfillment, support, creator accuracy |

Comments are evidence, not a mechanism to manufacture engagement. Code them as question, objection, comparison, intent, complaint, or support request.

### 变更退回与闭环

商品/Offer、主张或权利变更时，由该事实/权利负责人指出变更字段、证据、新版本及影响用途；内容/合作负责人沿上述绑定识别受影响的 content、brief、collaboration 和 use 记录，将旧版标为“不适用于这项已变更用途、待重审”，退给相应负责人。保留旧版本和原始值，不静默覆盖，也不把无关商品、片段或用途一并作废。重新核对并收到接收回执之前，不把旧批准迁移到新版本；未触及的安全工作可继续。

交付、反馈、权利及退出回执必须回到同一商品/事实版本上下文。后续经济复盘引用各自费用行、同群同窗与退款成熟证据；此处不重写 T11 算术，不把自然、达人、付费结果相加称因果增量或商业赢家。具体市场资格、当前费用/履约时钟及平台生命周期缺证仍保持原限制。
