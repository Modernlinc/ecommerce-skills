# Compliance and approval gates

For Business Center members, partners, asset-level permissions, Finance separation, offboarding, and access incidents, also read [business-center-and-asset-governance.md](business-center-and-asset-governance.md). Its least-privilege workflow does not authorize a live account change.

## Always stop before these actions

Ask for explicit confirmation that names the target and scope before:

- login, identity verification, account/Business Center/Shop binding, or permission changes;
- publishing, deleting, editing, scheduling, or hiding a post/LIVE/listing;
- sending a creator/customer message, collaboration invitation, sample, coupon, or offer;
- creating, editing, pausing, or scaling a paid campaign or changing an ROI target/budget;
- price, promotion, inventory, warehouse, shipping, returns, payment, payout, or tax settings;
- reading, exporting, uploading, or transforming real customer, order, employee, or performance data;
- any Production, API key, secret, cookie, token, or live integration operation.

A plan or draft is not authorization to execute it.

## Reject and redirect

| Request pattern | Do not provide | Safe redirect |
|---|---|---|
| 养号、防封、一机一号一 IP、换号绕限制 | algorithm gaming or enforcement evasion | supported account setup, authentic use, account-health evidence, appeal path |
| 购买账号/Apple ID、刷机净化、指纹环境规避、借用身份 | account acquisition, device or identity evasion | user-owned eligible accounts/devices, truthful market/entity setup, official recovery and access controls |
| 互赞互评、刷粉、刷播放、包装评论区 | artificial engagement or deceptive social proof | genuine replies, VOC coding, moderation policy, service recovery |
| 指定五星、补偿换好评、伪造邮箱/订单、跨平台评价搬运 | review manipulation or laundered social proof | neutral feedback request, verified-purchase evidence, service recovery, platform appeal |
| 搬运、像素级模仿、去水印、二创避版权 | unauthorized reuse or evasion edits | abstract the hook/structure, create owned footage, obtain license/creator authorization |
| 保证爆、稳定出单、月入、固定利润 | outcome guarantees | hypothesis, baseline, range, uncertainty, evidence and stop conditions |
| 固定 ROI/预算/发帖量/达人阈值 | universal numeric prescriptions | contribution economics, current account recommendations, controlled tests |
| 伪造地区、身份、仓库、资质 | false operational information | eligible entity/market route or hold |

## Content checklist

Before a commercial asset is ready for human approval, confirm:

- product shown matches the listing and available variant;
- claims are true, supportable, market-appropriate, and not exaggerated;
- commercial disclosure plan is stated;
- footage, music, voice, likeness, trademark, and creator rights are documented;
- AI-generated or materially edited realistic media is labeled when required;
- price, coupon, delivery, guarantee, and availability statements match the destination;
- no fake reviews, undisclosed incentives, misleading urgency, or prohibited product cues.
