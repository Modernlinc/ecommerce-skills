## 完整指南稿 P1

**Title：** How to Measure a Drawer for Dividers | Drawerline

**Meta description：** Measure the usable space inside your drawer before choosing dividers. Check the placement distance, height and obstacles, then compare Drawerline sizes.

**H1：** Measure Your Drawer Before Choosing Dividers

### Start with the position, not the cabinet width

Decide where you want a divider to sit. Measure the usable distance between the inside surfaces it will span at that position. The outside width of the cabinet is not a substitute for this measurement.

### Check the height and anything in the way

Measure the available internal height where the divider will sit. Look for runners, raised sections or other obstacles that could affect placement. Drawerline Short and Long dividers are 6 cm high; checking a length range alone does not confirm that a divider will fit your drawer.

### Compare the available ranges

Drawerline Short adjusts from 27 to 38 cm. Drawerline Long adjusts from 38 to 52 cm. Compare your measurements with the range and height of the option you are considering. These ranges are not a guarantee of suitability for every drawer design.

For a side-by-side choice, see the <a href="https://drawerline.example/collections/drawer-dividers">Drawerline divider size options</a>. If you are considering the longer range, check the <a href="https://drawerline.example/products/divider-long">Long divider dimensions and two-pack details</a> before buying.

### Can I cut a divider down to size?

No. The supplied product information says the dividers must not be cut shorter. Choose an option with a suitable stated adjustment range and check the drawer structure as well.

### Is a measurement inside the range enough?

No. You also need to check the usable height and anything that may obstruct the divider at its intended position. Do not assume that a matching distance proves compatibility with every drawer.
