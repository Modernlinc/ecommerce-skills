## 共用事实

CL-6-GREY 是一包六条灰色可重复开合线材标签，**整包 USD 12**。每条展开长 10 cm、宽 1.5 cm，不能把该尺寸写成包绕后的直径。每条有可擦写名字栏；盒内无笔，可使用自备水性可擦笔，写字和擦除效果随笔与使用条件变化。只用于断电且冷却的普通室内线材整理或标记，不是绝缘或承重部件。依据：CL-FACT-r1 第 1—3 项。

不采用耐用次数、防水、耐高温、节省时间或提升团队效率的主张，现有输入没有相应试验。只有对应条件明确的试验证据，才可能支持以后加入具体、有限定的表述。

## Instagram｜CL-IG-r1

重点是个人书桌上的充电线辨认。语气直接、日常；希望读者对照自己的标记需求与使用条件作判断。CTA 在正文内即可完成，不依赖未提供的链接或按钮。

### 完整英文文稿

Which cable is this?

Give a charging cable on your desk a written name with ClipLoop. Each grey label has an erasable name field and can be opened and closed again.

A pack contains six labels for USD 12 total. Each label measures 10 cm long × 1.5 cm wide when laid flat—not the diameter of a loop around a cable.

Bring your own water-based erasable pen; no pen is included. Writing and erasing results vary with the pen and conditions of use.

Use ClipLoop only to organize or mark ordinary indoor cables that are powered off and cool. The labels are not insulation or load-bearing parts.

Would a written cable name help you identify a cable at your desk? Compare your setup with the flat label size, pen requirement and use conditions above before deciding whether ClipLoop fits your needs.

### 完整英文文稿

At a shared desk, which device does this cable belong to?

ClipLoop gives you an erasable name field for a device or owner name on a cable label. The grey labels can be opened and closed again, making them an option to consider when planning how to mark cables at a shared workspace.

One pack contains six labels for USD 12 total. Each label is 10 cm long × 1.5 cm wide when laid flat; these measurements do not describe the diameter around a cable.

You will need your own water-based erasable pen. No pen is included, and writing and erasing results depend on the pen and conditions of use.

These labels are only for organizing or marking ordinary indoor cables that are powered off and cool. They are not insulation or load-bearing parts.

Think about one shared desk you use: which cables would need a device or owner name? Check the label size, pen requirement and use conditions against that setup before deciding whether ClipLoop belongs in it.
