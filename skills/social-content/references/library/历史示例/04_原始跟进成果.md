ClipLoop is a grey cable label with an erasable name field that can be opened and closed again. One pack contains six labels for USD 12 total. Each label measures 12 cm long × 1.5 cm wide when laid flat; that is not the diameter of a loop around a cable.

You can use your own water-based erasable pen. No pen is included, and writing and erasing results vary with the pen and conditions of use.

Use ClipLoop only to organize or mark ordinary indoor cables that are powered off and cool. The labels are not insulation or load-bearing parts.

Tell us which cable you would want to mark at your own desk, and what you would like to know before using ClipLoop on it. A cable description is enough—no names or personal details needed.
